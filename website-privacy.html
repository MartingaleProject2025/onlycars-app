OnlyCars Website Privacy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This page covers the onlycars-app.com website only. We count how many people visit and roughly where they come from. No cookies, no accounts, no advertising, and nothing that identifies you personally.

Which policy applies to what

  • This page covers this website.
  • The OnlyCars Privacy Policy covers the iPhone app, which does collect more in order to sync your garage. Nothing on this page changes that.

What we collect

We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits in aggregate. For each page view it records:

  • the page you viewed, and the date and time,
  • the referring website, if your browser sends one,
  • country, derived from your IP address,
  • browser, operating system, device type, and screen size,
  • any campaign tags in the link you followed, such as utm_source.

We also record when someone taps a link out to the App Store, so we can tell whether the site is doing its job. That event stores the page it happened on and the channel you arrived from, nothing else.

What we do not collect

  • No cookies, and nothing is stored on your device except a note of which channel you arrived from, held only until you close the tab.
  • No personal data. We never see your name, email, or precise location.
  • Your IP address is not stored. Umami uses it to derive a country and to compute a rotating, hashed identifier so repeat views within a day are not double-counted. The address itself is discarded and the hash cannot be reversed or used to recognise you later.
  • No cross-site or cross-session tracking, no advertising profiles, and no data sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No account or sign-in exists anywhere on this website.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we stop before we start. Nothing is recorded, nothing is written to your device, and no campaign tag is added to App Store links, so an opted-out visit cannot reach Apple's campaign reporting either. Links keep working exactly as they otherwise would.

Analytics blockers are equally effective, and the site works normally with them on.

App Store links

When you tap through to the App Store, we add a short campaign tag to the link so Apple can tell us which channel a download came from. Apple reports this back to us only as aggregate counts. It contains no information about you, and Apple's handling of your visit is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and consent

This website sets no cookies and collects no personal data, so there is no consent banner to click through. That is deliberate.

Children

Nothing on this website is directed at children, and because we collect no personal data we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Your rights

Because the data is aggregate and never tied to a person, there is no record of you to access, correct, or delete. Questions about any of this go to support@koalaloop.games.
